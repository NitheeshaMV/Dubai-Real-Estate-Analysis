Where do apartments pay back fastest? Rental yield by area, before and after service charges

Gross = median annual rent per sq ft of new leases () ÷ median sale price per sq ft of ready apartments, using only buildings that appear in both — so rent and price come from the same towers. Areas with 3+ buildings, 50+ ready sales and 50+ leases. Net = rent minus the 2026 RERA-approved service charge (DLD Service Charge Index) of the area's most-leased building, ÷ price; vacancy, maintenance and fees are not deducted. The chart shows the areas with a service-charge rate; the table lists every area. Not affected by the filters.
